Frequently Asked Questions
Important clarifications on clinical protocols, donor privacy, and matching rules.

Mandatory laboratory cross-matching, antibody screening, infectious marker tests, and clinical donor physical evaluation must always be conducted by qualified medical professionals before any transfusion.

BloodLink AI enforces strict zero-data-leakage policies. Exact coordinates and residential addresses of donors are never publicly displayed. Only anonymized distance calculations are performed, and emergency contact alerts are dispatched only with explicit notification consent.

Donors cannot self-verify donations. Only authorized personnel at accredited blood banks can verify in-person donations through their secure dashboard. Upon verification, the ledger automatically credits points transactionally to prevent duplicate rewards.

In accordance with WHO and Indian National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC) guidelines, a mandatory minimum cooldown interval of 56 days (8 weeks) is enforced between whole blood donations to ensure donor hemoglobin recovery and physical safety.
