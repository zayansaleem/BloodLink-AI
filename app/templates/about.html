Project Overview

About BloodLink AI

Engineered for the Royal Hackathon 2026 to bridge critical delays in emergency transfusion logistics.

The Challenge

In acute surgical, obstetric, and trauma emergencies, blood supply chains suffer from severe fragmentation. Medical teams often spend vital hours making frantic phone calls to multiple regional facilities, unaware of real-time inventory balances or nearby willing donors.

Our Solution

BloodLink AI unifies verified blood banks, authorized hospital request coordinators, and voluntary donors into an interoperable network. By combining server-side emergency prioritization scoring with explainable multi-factor donor matching, the platform ensures rapid, secure, and privacy-preserving response times.

Core Architecture Principles
Explainable AI Triage Prioritizes emergency requests using clear mathematical formulas (clinical severity, deadlines, shortage index) without black-box opacity.
Privacy-By-Design Donors are never exposed by exact home location. Communications occur through server-authorized notifications.
Verified Donation Rewards Incentivizes altruistic voluntary donation with verified points and coupons, never cash payments.
Strict Clinical Safeguards All organization accounts require administrator approval. Unverified organizations cannot post trusted requests.
