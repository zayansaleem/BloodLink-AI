How BloodLink AI Works

From verified hospital request to donor coordination, verification, and reward redemption.

1
Hospital Emergency Request

Authorized hospital clinical staff submit a blood request specifying blood group, component type, units required, location, and urgency.

2
Request Verification & Priority Calculation

Clinical coordinators verify the request authenticity. The AI Prioritization Engine calculates a transparent score ($0-100$) based on urgency, deadline, and regional shortages.

3
Blood Bank Inventory Search

The platform automatically queries verified regional blood bank inventories to check if stock can be immediately transferred to the patient hospital.

4
Preliminary Smart Donor Matching

If additional units are needed, the matching engine identifies suitable voluntary donors using ABO rules, Haversine proximity, 56-day cooldown intervals, and consent settings.

5
Donation Verification & Rewards

The donor donates in-person at the designated facility. Authorized staff verify the donation record, crediting Lifesaver Points to the donor's ledger for coupon redemption.
