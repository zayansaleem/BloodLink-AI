Voluntary blood donation is safe, regulated, and verified on-site by accredited medical staff.

Step 01

Clinical Criteria

WHO & NBTC Clinical Eligibility Guidelines

Voluntary blood donation is safe and healthy for adults who satisfy core physiological criteria. Certified medical teams perform hemoglobin tests and vitals checks prior to every collection.

Age & Body Weight: Aged 18 to 65 years with a minimum body weight of 45 kg (50 kg for apheresis/platelets).
Mandatory 56-Day Cooldown: At least 8 weeks (56 days) must elapse between whole blood donations to ensure red cell recovery.
Hemoglobin & General Health: Hemoglobin level ≥ 12.5 g/dL, normal blood pressure, and free from active infections or fever.
Medical Protocol Notice

Important Notice: Registration on BloodLink AI establishes your availability for emergency coordination. It does not certify medical eligibility. Certified pathologists and blood bank medical officers conduct mandatory screening tests prior to any donation.
